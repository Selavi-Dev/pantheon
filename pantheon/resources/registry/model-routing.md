# Model routing
Utility: fast extraction and classification. Professional: reliable tool use and synthesis. Reasoning: complex decisions and architecture. Independent reasoning: separate provider/model for high-stakes review where possible. Creative multimodal: vision and creative generation. See agents.json for per-agent defaults and escalations.

Use `hermes -p pantheon-<agent> model` or setup to select a provider and actual supported model. No provider is assumed and no automatic tier switching is implemented. Escalate manually or use a separately configured profile; do not claim escalation succeeded without evidence. Prefer two providers; measure cost/latency and do not substitute model diversity for independent evidence. Keep benchmark competitors isolated until submissions lock.
